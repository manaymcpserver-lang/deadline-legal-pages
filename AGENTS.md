## Website language coverage

The user requires every new or translated guide to be published in every website language listed in `app/guides/locales.json` (currently 47 variants). Do not choose a smaller priority-language batch or use English cards as a substitute for complete article translations. Translate the full body, metadata, steps, FAQs and interface labels, preserve factual product limits, review the translation set, and validate same-page language links and RTL layouts before publication. The search-guide build enforces full locale coverage.
