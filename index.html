How it works

Three steps. Then it fades into the background.

You don’t need a new system. Nonlate listens to the tools you already trust and speaks up at the one moment most productivity apps miss: right before you disappear into your phone.

First
Connect your sources
Pick from Notion, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Classroom, Microsoft To Do, Teams, Outlook, Jira, Trello, and Blackboard iCal.
Then
Choose your distractions
Mark the handful of apps that usually win: social, games, video, or whatever the honest answer is. Everything else stays untouched.
Finally
Get a quiet nudge
When you open a distraction, Nonlate pauses the moment and shows the next thing that’s actually due. You can continue anytime, and Nonlate does not log browsing history.
What you get

A gentler kind of productive.

Nonlate isn’t a task manager trying to replace your life. It’s a thin, respectful layer that makes the tools you already use feel like they’re finally on your side.

The intercept moment
A full-screen pause in front of your worst time-sinks. It shows your most urgent deadline with source, due time, and time left. Tap to work on it, or tap to continue.
Unified deadline view
Every task from every tool in one clean, honest list, grouped by what’s actually urgent instead of alphabetical order or project.
Real-time sync
Silent push from Notion, Google Calendar, Jira, Trello and more. When your deadline moves, Nonlate already knows.
You stay in control
Every nudge is skippable. Nonlate doesn’t lock you out, report on you, or moralize. It respects that some days a scroll is the right call.
Built for the way you actually live
Students juggling Classroom and Blackboard. Designers living in Notion. Engineers in Jira. Parents on Google Calendar. Nonlate gathers the deadlines so the next step is easier to see.

Privacy

Your deadlines. Not our product.

Nonlate is designed so the smallest possible amount of your life ever leaves your phone. It avoids behavior dashboards, selling, and profile building.

  • Minimum-needed OAuth scopes. Each integration asks only for what’s required to read your tasks and deadlines.
  • Tokens stay encrypted. On-device AES-256 storage. Certificate-pinned transport. Nothing sits in plaintext.
  • No cross-site tracking. No third-party analytics following you around the web.
  • One-tap disconnect. Every integration is reversible from settings. Uninstall wipes local data.
  • Email-based deletion. Request complete removal any time. Read deletion details.
