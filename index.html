Privacy by design

Connected data is for your deadlines—not ad targeting or AI training.

Nonlate asks for the data needed to sync the services you choose, protects credentials according to platform best practices, and gives you disconnect and deletion controls.

Read the full privacy policy →
01

Protected in transit and at rest

Data travels over HTTPS/TLS. Android credentials use EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Keystore; iOS credentials use Keychain.

02

Encrypted proxy secrets

When a provider’s webhook flow requires the proxy to retain a token or webhook secret, production secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before database storage.

03

Limited use of Google data

Google user data supports connected sync and focus features. It is not used for advertising or to train generalized AI models.

04

Your connections stay reversible

Disconnect integrations in settings or request deletion through Nonlate support.

Questions, answered plainly

Before Nonlate steps in.

The important details without the productivity theater.

Is Nonlate a reminder or a blocker?+

Both. Reminder shows due work and lets you decide. Blocker can require action or a timed break. Focus Lock adds a stronger reflection step.

Does Nonlate replace my task apps?+

No. It gathers deadlines from supported services, lets you add manual tasks, and turns that shared due-work context into plans, reminders, and focus rules.

Does every integration update instantly?+

No. Update speed varies by provider and plan. Free refreshes when you open or manually refresh, Plus adds hourly background sync, and Pro adds Live Sync where supported.

Does blocking work identically on iOS and Android?+

The deadline-aware focus experience exists on both platforms, but permissions and system-level interception behavior differ because iOS and Android provide different controls.

What does Nonlate do with connected data?+

It uses connected task and calendar data to provide sync, planning, reminders, and focus features. The full categories and controls are described in the Privacy Policy.
